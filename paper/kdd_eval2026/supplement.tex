\section{Baseline Fairness Ledger}
\label{app:fairness}

No-Evolution, One-Shot, and Code-Evol are native to this harness and omitted.
All systems share the identical task stream, seed library, model (Sonnet~4),
and verification function; only the tool-creation protocol varies.

\section{Skill-Bundle Evaluation}
\label{app:skill}
To extend the benchmark from bare-function evaluation to multi-file \emph{skill
bundles} (the format used by Anthropic Claude Skills and
CoEvoSkills~\cite{zhang2026coevoskills}), we wrap every preserved tool into a
4-file bundle (\texttt{SKILL.md}, function, tests, \texttt{metadata.json}) and
score three bundle-level dimensions: Structure ($S$), Documentation ($D$), and
Metadata ($M$). Bundle quality averages tool TQS with $S$, $D$, $M$.

\section{Per-Domain Analysis}
\label{app:domain}
Tool-evolution effectiveness varies substantially by problem type, a dimension
invisible to aggregate TC.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{llcccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Domain} & \textbf{Model} & \textbf{TC} & \textbf{Tools} & \textbf{Reuse} & \textbf{LH} \\
\midrule
Data Trans. & Sonnet & .573 & 14 & .734 & .476 \\
Data Trans. & Haiku & .600 & 7 & .800 & .500 \\
\midrule
API Orch. & Sonnet & .909 & 4 & .667 & .778 \\
API Orch. & Haiku & 1.00 & 3 & .333 & .667 \\
\midrule
Numerical & Sonnet & .651 & 4 & .667 & .500 \\
Numerical & Haiku & .621 & 3 & .556 & .481 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Code-Evol results by domain. API orchestration yields highest library
health; data transformation creates the most tools but with lower quality.}
\label{tab:domain_supp}
\end{table}

\paragraph{API orchestration: clean, composable problems.} Highest TC (up to 100\%) and library health (77.8\%), though based on a single session ($N{=}11$ tasks). HMAC authentication and pagination are clean, single-responsibility functions well-suited to tool creation.

\paragraph{Data transformation: high tool creation, lower quality.} Creates the most tools (14 on Sonnet) but achieves only 47.6\% library health: many synthesised parsers for proprietary binary formats fail held-out tests while passing self-generated tests.

\paragraph{Numerical computation: consistently difficult.} Both models achieve similar results ($\sim$63--65\% TC, $\sim$48--50\% LH), suggesting these tasks challenge the LLM regardless of model capability.
